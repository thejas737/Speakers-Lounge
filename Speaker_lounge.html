The Philosophy

SPEAKERS LOUNGE is a club that drops you into real-world scenarios where public speaking and effective communication are mandated.

Scenarios such as project proposals, shark-tank pitches, dealing with the paparazzi, debating a hot-topic, navigating board-room chaos, or teaching your friend through simple analogies.

Our ultimate goal is to build raw confidence within individuals to break out of their comfort zone and speak their ideas out under extreme pressure.

Gamified Sessions

High-stakes, absurd, and intense. Step into roles, pitch crazy ideas, and survive the interrogation.

Intermediate

Delusionism 101

Assume a wild title—CEO, President, Millionaire. Enact the role under pressure. CEO taking an opportunity call? President facing a threat? It's all high-stakes improv.

Expert

3AM_Shower_Tank

Pitch a wild product in 4+2 mins. Protein beer? Toilets with disco? Take those crazy 3AM ideas and convince the "sharks" they are the future.

Expert

Sit-down Comedy

Prepare a stand-up speech based on hilarious pre-assigned scenarios. Publicly apologize for an unskippable ad or prove a conspiracy to clowns.

Expert

ViralBiriyani (Mock-Press)

Face the paparazzi. Adopt a persona and survive a rapid-fire pressure test from the jury asking "difficult" and hostile questions.

Beginner

Friction (Debate)

Form groups and debate the hot-topic of the day. Defend your stance, clash with the opposition, and persuade the room.

All Levels

Pointblank (Podcast)

Grab a chai and converse. Relevant discussions with famous personalities in and around college, recorded for our Instagram platform.

Beginner

Domino

Pass the story. Start with a word/sentence and pass it along. Watch the narrative unfold as members blurt out their thoughts in a snap.

Intermediate

Prop-aganda

Prop-based storytelling. Pick a random item and generate a compelling 5-minute story on the spot while holding the object.

Beginner

Koko

Would-you-rather divide. The room splits based on a choice. If the random ball lands on you, justify your choice in exactly ONE sentence.

Beginner

The Jargon Filter

Explain complex technical topics purely using external analogies. Teach semiconductor bands via traffic jams, or SSDs via kitchen prep.

Beginner

Mafia

A project is ruined. Cross-examine the room to find the saboteur. Defend yourself calmly from false accusations or lie convincingly. Traitors-style.

Professional Sessions

Navigate board-room chaos, deliver difficult news, and assert dominance in corporate environments.

01

Executive Briefing

Learn from experts on pitching business proposals, making on-spot decisions, gathering analogies, and navigating aggressive Q&A. Show off your product in style.

02

The Art of Diplomatic Communication

Deliver sensitive news while protecting relationships. Master word-choices, framing, and learn how to say "NO" in the corporate world without offending authorities.

03

Mastering Virtual Presence

Command authority on a video call. Manage digital body language and keep a distracted remote audience fully engaged through a screen.
